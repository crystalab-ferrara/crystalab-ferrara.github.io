Cleanroom

130 m² cleanroom

The controlled environment where every crystal is made.

Work with us on this instrument

What it does

130 m² of filtered, controlled air, where dust cannot settle on wafers between steps. It houses the dicing, lithography and bonding equipment, the spin coater and the wet benches for chemical etching.

Keeping the chain in one place matters: a crystal goes from dicing to etching, coating, patterning and bonding without leaving the controlled environment, and is checked in our metrology labs at each stage.

What we use it for

  • Every fabrication step

    From raw wafer to bonded optic, for all our research lines.

  • Students at work

    Bachelor, master and PhD students learn cleanroom practice on real projects.

Working with us

Researchers and companies can work with us in the cleanroom on joint projects: write to us with what you need to make.
